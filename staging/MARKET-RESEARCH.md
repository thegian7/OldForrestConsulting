| Tier | Hourly Rate | Daily Rate | Notes |
|------|-------------|------------|-------|
| Junior / Entry-level | $100-150/hr | $600-900/day | Implementation support, basic integrations |
| Mid-level / Independent | $150-300/hr | $1,000-2,000/day | Complex AI projects, architecture |

| Model | Typical Range | Best For |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| Project-based (small strategy) | $5,000-25,000 | AI readiness assessments, PoC scoping |
| Project-based (implementation) | $50,000-500,000+ | Full AI system build-outs |
| Retainer (essential advisory) | $2,000-5,000/mo (5-10 hrs) | Ongoing guidance, architecture review |
| Retainer (standard support) | $5,000-15,000/mo (10-25 hrs) | Active project oversight + hands-on |

### OFC Positioning

Based on the website's pricing page and service offerings, OFC likely targets the mid-level independent tier ($150-300/hr). For a small firm, retainer models ($3,000-10,000/mo) provide the most predictable revenue. The assumption audit correctly flags that lead flow — not proposal speed — is the bottleneck.

## Client Acquisition Channels

### What Works for AI Consulting Firms

| Channel | Effectiveness | Cost | Notes |
|---------|---------------|------|-------|
| **Referrals & word-of-mouth** | Highest conversion | Free | Requires existing client base. #1 channel for solo consultants |
| **Content marketing / thought leadership** | High, slow build | Time-intensive | Blog posts, LinkedIn articles, case studies. Compounds over time |
| **LinkedIn outreach** | Medium-high | $0-2,000/mo tools | Personalized DMs to decision-makers. Works for B2B |
| **Speaking / webinars** | High credibility | Time + travel | Conference talks, podcast appearances, hosted webinars |
| **SEO / organic search** | Medium, very slow | $0-500/mo | "AI consulting [city]" or "[industry] AI solutions" |
| **Paid ads (Google/LinkedIn)** | Low ROI for services | $2,000-10,000/mo | CPA for consulting leads is $200-500+. Only viable at higher ACV |
| **Clutch / UpWork / Toptal** | Medium for early clients | 10-20% platform fee | Good for building portfolio and testimonials |
| **Partnerships** | High value, hard to build | Relationship investment | Tech vendors (cloud providers, AI tool companies) refer clients |

## Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors (Small AI Consulting)

The AI consulting market is highly fragmented at the small-firm level. No dominant player exists in the "1-5 person AI consulting firm" segment. Competition comes from:

1. **Solo AI consultants on Toptal/Upwork**: Compete on price ($75-200/hr), mostly project-based
2. **Boutique AI firms** (10-50 people): Higher rates ($200-400/hr), industry specialization
3. **Big 4 / enterprise firms**: $400-500+/hr, minimum $100K engagements. Not competing for the same clients
4. **AI tool vendors offering "services"**: ElevenLabs, OpenAI, Anthropic partner programs. Growing channel conflict

### Differentiation Opportunities
- **Industry specialization**: Healthcare AI, legal AI, manufacturing AI — specialists command 30-50% premiums
- **Outcome-based pricing**: "Pay for results" models are emerging but rare. High risk, high reward
- **AI-native operations**: Using AI tools internally (proposal generation, project management) as a proof point

## Regulatory Considerations

- **No licensing required** for AI consulting in most US states
- **E&O insurance**: Recommended ($1-3M coverage, ~$2,000-5,000/yr premium). Some enterprise clients require it
- **Data handling**: If accessing client data for AI projects, must comply with relevant regulations (HIPAA for healthcare, SOC 2 for enterprise, GDPR for EU clients)
- **AI-specific regulation**: EU AI Act (effective 2025-2026) creates consulting opportunities for EU-facing clients needing compliance help
- **Non-compete / IP concerns**: Standard MSA should address IP ownership of AI models trained on client data
