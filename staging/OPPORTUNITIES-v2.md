# Old Forrest Consulting — Revised Monetization Opportunities (v2)

**Revision date:** 2026-03-20
**Based on:** Assumption audit + market research findings

---

## Reality Check

OFC is the most grounded project in the portfolio. It's a real business with real services, real pricing ($150-300/hr, $1.5K-5K/mo retainer, $3K-20K projects), real team (2 people), and a live website. The revenue estimates in v1 were reasonable in concept but inflated because they assumed traffic that doesn't exist.

**Critical insight from the audit:** Every opportunity below depends on traffic/lead flow. Without knowing current baseline traffic, conversion improvements are meaningless. If the site gets 200 visitors/month, even 2x conversion = 2 extra leads, not $50K.

**Market context:** Fractional CTO demand is surging (72% of CEOs plan to increase use of fractional execs). AI specialists command 30%+ premiums. The market is fragmented at the small-firm level — no dominant player below $10K/mo. This is the right market at the right time.

---

## Opportunity Rankings (Revised)

### 1. Content Marketing: SEO Blog + LinkedIn Outreach — **$30K-100K/yr (Year 2+)**

**Revised from:** $50K-150K/yr
**Revenue model:** Inbound lead generation driving consulting engagements
**Priority: HIGHEST — everything else depends on this**

**Why this is #1 now (moved up from #3):** The assumption audit identified the chicken-and-egg problem: optimizing conversion (ROI calculator, case studies) only matters if there's traffic. Currently there is no blog, no SEO, no content marketing. This is the bottleneck.

**Revised projections:**
- SEO for B2B consulting takes 6-12 months to generate meaningful leads (not 3-6 months as originally stated)
- LinkedIn content + direct outreach delivers faster (1-3 months to first lead)
- Realistic Year 1: 5-10 inbound leads from content
- At 20% close rate and $10K average engagement: $10K-20K attributable revenue
- Year 2 (compounding): $30K-100K as content library grows

**Customer acquisition strategy (prioritized):**
1. **LinkedIn content + direct outreach** — fastest path to leads. 2-3 posts/week on AI project topics. Personalized DMs to decision-makers.
2. **2-3 case study blog posts** optimized for "AI project rescue [industry]," "fix stalled AI project," "fractional CTO for startups"
3. **Keyword validation needed:** Research actual search volume for target terms before investing heavily. "AI project rescue" may have low search volume, not low competition.
4. **Speaking at local tech meetups / podcasts** — builds credibility and generates referrals
5. **Referral program with existing clients** (see Opportunity 4)

**Effort estimate (revised):** 2-3 weeks for blog infrastructure + first 3 posts. Ongoing: 1-2 posts/month.

**Competitive positioning:** AI consulting market is fragmented at the small-firm level. OFC's differentiator is AI-native operations (the bookwriting system, video pipelines, etc. are proof points). Content should showcase this.

### 2. ROI Calculator Widget — **$10K-30K/yr (attributable)**

**Downgraded from:** $50K-100K/yr
**Revenue model:** Conversion rate optimization on existing (and future) traffic
**Priority: HIGH — quick win, 3-5 days of work**

**Why downgraded:** The original estimate assumed meaningful traffic. A calculator on a site with 200 visitors/month adds maybe 1-2 leads/month. However, it's still the best ROI per hour of development time because:
- Low effort (3-5 days, static export compatible)
- Becomes more valuable as SEO/content drives traffic
- Shareable as standalone content (embed on LinkedIn, share in DMs)

**Revised projections:**
- Year 1 (low traffic): 2-5 extra leads = $10K-30K attributable
- Year 2 (with SEO traffic): 5-15 extra leads = $30K-75K attributable

**Implementation:** React component showing "cost of delay vs. cost of rescue" and "DIY hours vs. expert hours." No backend needed.

### 3. Case Study Enhancement with ROI Metrics — **$10K-25K/yr (attributable)**

**Downgraded from:** $30K-50K/yr
**Revenue model:** Improved conversion through social proof

**Why downgraded:** Case studies already exist and are detailed. Adding explicit ROI numbers ("saved $X," "launched Y weeks early") is valuable but requires client permission to share metrics. The filterable gallery is a nice UX improvement.

**Key question:** Does OFC have permission to share quantified ROI from past engagements? If yes, this is 3-5 days of work with real conversion impact. If no, it's just a UI enhancement.

**Effort estimate:** 3-5 days (unchanged, estimate was accurate)

---

### 4. Referral Program — **$15K-40K/yr**

**Revised from:** $25K-50K/yr
**Revenue model:** Revenue share with referrers (10-15% of first engagement)

**Why this matters:** For solo/small consulting firms, referrals are the #1 client acquisition channel. Formalizing word-of-mouth is high-leverage.

**Revised projections:**
- Requires an existing network of past clients, partners, developers
- Realistic: 3-5 referral-sourced engagements per year
- At $5K-10K average engagement value: $15K-50K/yr
- Net after referral fee (10-15%): $13K-42K/yr

**Customer acquisition strategy:**
- Email past clients with referral program announcement
- Offer 10-15% of first engagement value
- Simple tracking (unique referral codes/links)
- Quarterly check-ins with referral partners

**Effort estimate (revised):** 2-3 weeks (original: 1-2 weeks). Tracking system + payout tracking + referral partner outreach.

### 5. Booking & Scheduling Enhancement — **$5K-15K/yr (attributable)**

**Downgraded from:** $20K-40K/yr
**Revenue model:** Reduced friction in lead-to-call conversion

**Why downgraded:** The current Google Calendar embed works. Upgrading to Cal.com or Calendly with intake questionnaire and reminders is a good improvement but won't generate $20K-40K unless there's high booking volume to optimize.

**Effort estimate:** 3-5 days (unchanged, accurate)

**Verdict:** Quick win, do it alongside the ROI calculator.

---

### 6. AI-Powered Proposal Generator — **DEFERRED**

**Downgraded from:** $100K-200K/yr
**Revenue model:** Faster close rates (internal tool)

**Why deferred:**
- The assumption audit correctly identified: if the bottleneck is lead flow (it is), faster proposals don't help
- Requires switching from static export to a dynamic architecture (or building a separate service)
- 2-3 weeks for a quality AI proposal generator is an MVP at best
- Should only be built after lead flow is established and proposal volume justifies automation

**Revisit when:** OFC is handling 5+ proposals/month and proposal turnaround is a demonstrable bottleneck.

---

### 7. Client Portal — **DEFERRED**

**Downgraded from:** $75K-150K/yr
**Revenue model:** Client retention and upsell

**Why deferred:**
- A portal doesn't create revenue; it reduces churn
- Only valuable with 5+ active retainer clients
- 4-6 weeks for MVP is a significant investment
- Build when client volume justifies it, not before

**Revisit when:** OFC has 5+ concurrent retainer clients.

### 8. Client NPS/Survey System — **$5K-10K/yr (attributable)**

**Downgraded from:** $10K-20K/yr
**Revenue model:** Testimonial generation + churn prevention

**Effort estimate:** 2-3 days (unchanged). Use Typeform or similar — don't build custom.

### Phase 3: Ongoing (Months 2-6)

- LinkedIn outreach to decision-makers

## The Honest Take

OFC is a real business in a growing market at the right time. The fractional CTO + AI consulting positioning is strong. But the website is currently a brochure, not a lead generation engine. The single highest-ROI investment is content marketing (blog + LinkedIn). Everything else is optimization on a funnel that needs traffic first.

**If Tom can write 1-2 LinkedIn posts per week and 1-2 blog posts per month for 6 months, the organic lead pipeline alone will likely exceed $100K/yr in attributed revenue by month 12.**
